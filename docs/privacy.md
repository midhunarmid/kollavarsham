# Privacy policy

_Draft, Day 2. Final text is reviewed by the owner before it is published on `calendar.armid.in` (Day 22) and linked from the Play listing (Day 18)._

**Malayalam Calendar** (Android app `in.armid.calendar`, and the web version at calendar.armid.in) is made by Midhun Mohanan ("we", "the developer").

## Summary

- **We do not collect any data.** The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no servers of ours, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising.
- **Everything stays on your device.** Your people, events, reminders, settings and any photos you add are stored only on the device or in your browser.
- **The app works offline.** It does not need an internet connection to show dates, panchangam or festivals.

## What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following on your device. It never sends them anywhere.

| Data | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|
| People you add (name, relation, colour, optional photo) | to show their pirannal, nakshatram and birthdays | local database on the device |
| Events and reminder settings | to schedule notifications | local database on the device |
| Your settings (language, location you choose, theme) | to show dates correctly | local preferences on the device |
| Location, if you enter one or allow the device location | to compute sunrise, sunset and month starts | used on the device only; not sent anywhere |

Location is optional. You can choose a Kerala city from a list, or type a place and time zone yourself. If you allow device location, it is read on the device to compute sunrise and sunset, and it is not stored on our side or sent to anyone.

## Permissions the app asks for

- **Notifications** (Android 13 and later): to show your reminders. You can refuse, and the app keeps working without them.
- **Exact alarms**: to deliver reminders at the time you choose. If you refuse, reminders may arrive a few minutes late.
- **Location** (optional): see above.
- **Photos or files** (optional): only when you choose a person's photo or import or export a backup file. The app reads or writes the file you pick.
- **Internet**: the app does not use the internet to show dates. Web-only features (for example, opening the Play store page) use a link that you choose to follow.

## Backups

- You can export a backup file (JSON) and an `.ics` calendar file. These files are created on your device and shared only when you choose to share them.
- Android Auto Backup may copy the app's data to your Google account backup, under Android's own settings. We do not control that backup and do not receive it.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app starts to collect any data, this policy will be updated before the change is released, and the update will say so at the top.
