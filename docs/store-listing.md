## Full description

### English

> The Malayalam calendar for Kerala, computed on your phone.
>
> **Pirannal reminders.** Add your family's birth stars: "Pooradam in Dhanu". The app finds the day every year, even when the star falls twice in the month, and reminds you. It can also remind you every month when the star returns (nakshatram), and on Gregorian birthdays, anniversaries and shraddham (by tithi or star). Don't know the star? "Find my star" works it out from the date and time of birth.
>
> **Today and every day.** Malayalam and English dates, star and tithi with their end times, sunrise and sunset, Rahu kalam, Gulika kalam and Yamagandam, yoga, karana, moonrise and moonset.
>
> **Festivals and holidays.** Onam, Vishu, Thrissur Pooram, Sivaratri, Navaratri, Deepavali, Christmas, Easter, Eid and more, with Kerala government holidays. Filter by category, search the agenda, and read what each festival is and how its date is found.
>
> **Your calendar, your way.** Month view in Malayalam months or wall-calendar style, a year view, Malayalam or English, Malayalam digits, light and dark themes, your city in Kerala (or anywhere), and the North Malabar year starting in Kanni.
>
> **Tools.** Date converter, age calculator, days between dates, the months of any Kollavarsham year, and share cards of the day's panchangam or a pirannal greeting.
>
> **Private.** No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone; back up to a file when you want, or export dates to Google Calendar.
>
> Found a date that doesn't match your almanac? Tap "Report a wrong date" on that day.

## Data safety form

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does the app collect or share any of the required user data types? | **No** |

Notes for the reviewer: people, events and settings are stored on the device. Backups and `.ics` files are created on the device and shared only through the system share sheet when the user chooses. "Report a wrong date" opens a GitHub page in the browser with the date pre-filled; nothing is sent unless the user submits the issue there.

## Permissions declared

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | reminders |
| `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` | reminders at the chosen time; the app falls back to inexact alarms if refused |
| `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` | restore scheduled reminders after a restart |

Release builds have no `INTERNET` permission: the app works offline, and links (the GitHub report, the website) open in the browser.

The exact-alarm declaration form in Play Console: "Calendar app: reminders for user-created events at user-chosen times."
