# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased; first Play release)

- Today, Calendar (Malayalam or wall-calendar months), year view and Day detail with the full panchangam: star, tithi, yoga and karana with end times, sun and moon times, Rahu kalam, Gulika and Yamagandam.
- Festivals computed by the engine, merged with Kerala's holiday lists; six category filters; an agenda with search; festival pages with description, rule and next dates.
- People and their events: pirannal, nakshatram, birthday, shraddham by tithi or star, anniversary and custom rules; "Find my star".
- Reminders: Android notifications (exact when allowed), daily panchangam, festival and month-start reminders, Snooze; a daily background refresh; on the web, a reminder list and `.ics` export.
- Tools: date converter, age, days between dates, Kollavarsham year; panchangam and greeting share cards.
- Backup and restore (JSON), `.ics` export, Android Auto Backup.
- Home-screen widgets (Today, Next pirannal), launcher shortcuts, "Report a wrong date".
- English and Malayalam, Malayalam digits, light and dark themes, 14 Kerala cities or any place, North Malabar (Kanni) year.
- Web app at calendar.armid.in/app/, installable and offline.
